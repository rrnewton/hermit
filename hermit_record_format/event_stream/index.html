normalize_unused_args
Returns syscall with any argument registers beyond its kernel arity zeroed. This makes the record/replay desync comparison ignore unused registers, which are not part of the syscall and may legitimately differ between record and replay. Syscalls without a known arity are returned unchanged (all six registers still compared).
