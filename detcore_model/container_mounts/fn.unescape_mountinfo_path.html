pub fn unescape_mountinfo_path(escaped: &[u8]) -> Option<Vec<u8>>

Undo the kernel’s mountinfo path escaping. Linux escapes exactly space, tab, newline and backslash as \040, \011, \012, \134 (mangle_path in fs/proc_namespace.c); anything else after a backslash is malformed and yields None, so callers enumerating mounts to act on can fail closed instead of acting on a wrong path.
