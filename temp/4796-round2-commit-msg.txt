4796: keep the uncovered interval after a recovered re-arm, keep the hit budget per thread

[hermit2, musetrain, tbh-1005, devbig014, role=impl]

Plain Language Summary and Project Impact

Round 2 of the blocked re-arm reporting fix. If the new image later
unblocked SIGTRAP and armed again, the resumed observation overwrote
the blocked status and the uncovered interval vanished from the trace
and the warning. Separately, closing an interval had reset the
thread's first-64/last-64 hit budget, so one thread could record 128
hits per interval instead of 128 in total. The watch ledger now holds
one hit budget and one bounded tail per thread for its whole life,
and coverage intervals (armed/uncovered spans with their reasons)
close at both edges of an uncovered span: at a failed re-arm and when
observation resumes. Earlier genuine arms and hits are preserved.

Determinism

Reporting only. Interval closing and the budget are pure functions of
the recorded watch-status and hit sequence per thread; summaries stay
in thread and interval order. No schedule, signal mask, virtual time
or guest-visible result changes, so the same recorded run writes the
same summaries and warning on every replay.

Linux Semantics

No guest semantics change: exec still keeps the blocked signal mask
and clears the debug registers, an unblock still lets the thread arm
again, and stores made while uncovered still run unwatched. Only
Hermit's coverage reporting and hit accounting change.
