Bind the genuine initial physical leader before its first thread-start. This does not initialize deterministic process identity or run a callback.

§Safety

The caller owns the actual authenticated InitialImage lifecycle and passes PID/TID read from that same current kernel task through its qualified gate. No guest work has run; this is the unique freshly constructed initial state.
