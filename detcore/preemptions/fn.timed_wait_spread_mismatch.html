Expand description

The refusal of a replay that would not redraw a record’s spread-wait factors, naming the record by source (an option and a path), or None when it would: the replay’s --chaos-timed-wait-spread (spread) must be the recorded one, and where both the record and the replay name a scheduler seed (seed), so must the seed. Absent and off are the same.
