pub fn format_without_background_starts(format: Option<u32>) -> Option<u32>
Expand description

The newest format a record may state when it does not hold every background start of its run: format, lowered below BACKGROUND_STARTS_FORMAT when it is a format this Hermit reads at or above it (BACKGROUND_STARTS_FORMAT..=PREEMPTION_RECORD_FORMAT_MAX). A schedule replay’s re-record, a slice-boundary recording, an edit or a shorter prefix of a schedule, and an analyze or bisect trial note no starts, so they keep the timing rules of their format and claim no list of starts. Any other stated format, newer than this Hermit or invalid (0), stays as stated, so that reading the record still refuses it (PreemptionRecord::validate_format): lowered, a newer Hermit’s record would read as one without what it may hold.
