Fix aliased-recvmsg io-buffer EFAULT; re-enable socket and procfs ptrace verify

[hermit2, degraded-unresolved, unresolved, devbig014, role=impl]

Plain Language Summary and Project Impact
c-programs/socket-timestamp-edge-cases crashed before comparison (verify
exit 125, guest exit 9): Detcore's io-buffer hashing re-read the guest
msghdr after recvmsg, but the guest legally points msg_control at its own
header, so the receive overwrote msg_iov with control bytes and hashing
faulted a successful syscall with EFAULT. Message-syscall iovecs are now
snapshotted before dispatch (recvmsg/sendmsg/recvmmsg/sendmmsg) and extents
resolve from the snapshot; batch messages whose msg_len was itself
overwritten contribute no extent instead of hashing unwritten bytes. The
cell re-qualified 20/20 canonical matched and is re-enabled for ptrace
(liteinst not re-run, stays ci:false). system-utils/procfs-sanitized-paths
re-qualified 20/20 under its pre-existing manifest comparison profile
(compare_io_buffers:false, rcb_time:false; the retained divergence was in
smaps raw bytes that profile excludes by design) and is re-enabled; plan,
DAG, and frozen counts are regenerated (+2 cells). The virtual-timerfd
scheduler change for timer-family-identity is a separate change with its
own deterministic-scheduling review and is not in this commit; that cell
stays ci:false.

Determinism
The snapshot is taken from the same guest state the kernel consumes, so
extent resolution is a pure function of pre-call headers, delivered byte
counts, and (batch) post-call msg_len; it cannot depend on control bytes
written over the header. No comparator or guest assertion changed.

Linux Semantics
msg_control overlapping msghdr is permitted; native recvmsg succeeds and
the guest's truncated/aliased/batch expectations all pass. Timestamp
canonicalization in handle_recvmsg is unchanged.

Validation
cargo test -p hermit-detcore --lib io_buffers: 14 passed including new
aliased-header regression. cargo test -p hermit-manifest-plan: all suites
passed. cargo fmt --check and clippy -p hermit-detcore: clean. Canonical
ptrace verify: socket guest 20/20 matched, procfs 20/20 matched under its
manifest profile. Independent adversarial review: no goalpost moving.
