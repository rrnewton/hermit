One first-party Detcore engine DSO for ordinary in-guest entry mechanisms.

No InitialImage, successor, native-exec or mapped-image entry is exported. Those issuers and affine authorities remain in the maintained leaves. This package cannot inherit their source or finite qualification proofs.
