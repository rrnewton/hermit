A saved-frame loader’s genuine post-load entry, before client constructors. The native stub retains only borrowed loader arguments, then establishes the same guarded stack/backing. The body copies settings before returning.

§Safety

Exact header-declared ordinary loader ABI; argv has argc valid NUL-terminated entries and the initial thread alone is live. No foreign seccomp filter may be present.
