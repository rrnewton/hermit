Hermit’s ptrace recording tool, Recorder, and the one place its Detcore instantiation is rooted.

detcore::Detcore<Recorder> drives reverie_ptrace’s TracedTask, and that instantiation is large. Compiled inside the hermit library it shared one LLVM codegen unit with every other backend’s instantiation; in its own crate it builds in a separate rustc process, beside the others. The entry point, spawn_tracer, is not generic, so hermit instantiates nothing of it.
