Whether hermit_args, an attempt’s argv before the guest separator, is an invocation the runner writes for a cell whose declared nonzero exit it checks: a verify cell’s comparison with --verify-allow=failure, or a replay cell’s record start, whose comparison takes --verify-allow=both (verified_invocation_argv). A reader re-deciding a declared exit from a retained row requires one of these.
