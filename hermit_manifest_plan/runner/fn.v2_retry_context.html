pub fn v2_retry_context(
    context: &RunContext,
    cell: &SelectedCell,
    oci_dir: &Path,
) -> Result<RunContext, Box<CellResult>>
Expand description

The context of cell’s v2 retry attempt inside the rootfs at oci_dir, or the typed refusal that stands for that attempt where the rootfs’s fixture verdict (v2_rootfs_missing) says it cannot load the cell’s fixture, or gives no usable verdict. A refusal launches nothing: its row is an ERROR with understood_prerequisite_failure, its error kind is ROOTFS_MISSING_SYMBOL or ROOTFS_UNCHECKED, and its reason names what the rootfs lacks, or why there is no verdict. Only the v2 retry consults the verdict; a host cell never does.
