pub fn diagnostic_failure_reason(history: &[CellResult]) -> Option<&str>
Expand description

The reason one cell’s attempt history ends in a NON-BLOCKING diagnostic failure, or None when it must count as an ordinary result.

Only a history whose LAST attempt is a measured FAIL of a diagnostic cell qualifies: a product failure, or the cell exceeding its own time budget (a timed-out compatibility probe was a product failure of its node before these probes became cells). A PASS is a pass; an ERROR, or a FAIL with any other no-result cause, keeps failing the run, including an ERROR retry after a product FAIL, as does a diagnostic row that lost its recorded reason.
