KVM cells whose qualification evidence needs a bound above the ordinary default. This is separate from the earlier full retained-data census so its source revision and sample count stay explicit. Three cells the 338-cell flip of 2026-10-30 made required with measured bounds above the ordinary default, from local samples on the host named in docs/TESTING_ENVIRONMENTS.md under “Named measurement hosts”, at the flip head (test-harness run, 2026-10-30); each cell was also CLEAN on the nightly pressure sweeps of 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30. Configured bounds follow the manifest-declared rules recorded in each cell’s reason: ceil(1.5x measured CPU) and ceil(3x measured wall). The third cell, python-io-subprocess-time, left the blocking validate on 2026-10-31 (deselected with python-dict-hash-iteration under the owner’s deselect-and-fix-forward rule after its CPU cost inside full validates reached 2.6x solo; root cause: https://github.com/rrnewton/hermit/issues/4996), so its calibration is removed with the cell; its measurements stay in that issue and in the manifest git history.
