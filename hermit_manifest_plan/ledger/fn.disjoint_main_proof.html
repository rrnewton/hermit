pub fn disjoint_main_proof(

Expand description

Prove from Git, in repo, that target may be admitted against the queued main queued (E) although main is now observed (M), failing closed on any error. list_text is the floor-sensitive list as read from the target’s tree (ci/admission/floor-sensitive-paths.txt). In order:

  1. E and M are distinct full SHAs, and so is the target, which is not E (a target equal to E is a main-ancestor run);
  2. E is an ancestor of the target and of M;
  3. M is not an ancestor of the target (that is a current-main run);
  4. the first-parent count n of E..M is 1..=12, and E is on M’s first-parent line (M~n is E), so a side-branch commit merged into main is never a floor;
  5. the paths changed between E and M and between E and the target (end trees, renames split) are disjoint;
  6. E..target changes no pin file (TARGET_PIN_FILE_PATTERNS);
  7. no commit in E..M touched a floor-sensitive path. This is the per-commit set, not the E-to-M tree diff: check-record-version-floor.rs reads the floor’s history (git log {floor} -- hermit-cli/src/metadata.rs), so a change made and reverted inside E..M still changes its verdict.
