Expand description

The one cause of a verdict other than matched or diverged: a closed set, with no umbrella or other class. The post-pass derives it from the operands’ typed result fields (CellResult::result, CellResult::outcome and CellResult::error_kind) and from which step failed, never from reason text; the record’s reason keeps the specific cause. Declared in the order the scorecard prints the classes, group by group (UnavailableClass::group).

Variants§

§

DeterminismMismatch

An operand’s verify cell diverged between its two runs on some attempt (its result determinism-failure), so it has no deterministic log. Only verdict nondeterministic.

§

Timeout

An operand’s final attempt timed out (result timeout). A row with exactly one attempt, whose strict comparison matched, is compared instead, unless its SaBRe execution path was ineligible.

§

Crash

An operand’s final attempt crashed or exited wrongly (result crash-error). A row with exactly one attempt, whose strict comparison matched, is compared instead, unless its SaBRe execution path was ineligible.

§

Oom

An operand’s final attempt ran out of memory (result oom).

§

InfrastructureError

An operand’s cell failed for an infrastructure cause (its own typed result infrastructure-error). A caller refusing the cell’s evidence is never this class (ParityRejection::typed).

§

SandboxDenied

A sandbox denied an operation an operand’s cell needed (result sandbox-denied).

§

FailedUntyped

An operand’s cell FAILed with no typed cause parity can name: no result, or a replay-failure or parity-failure result. The reason names the value.

§

Ended

An operand’s cell ended with an outcome other than PASS, FAIL or HOST-INAPPLICABLE, and no typed cause. The reason names the outcome and the error kind.

§

HostInapplicable

An operand’s cell was host-inapplicable, so it left no log: a HOST-INAPPLICABLE row, or a caller’s proof of the same (ParityRejection::HostInapplicable).

§

InputsNotEqualized

The backend cannot be given the reference’s inputs (ParityBackend::inputs_not_equalizable). Only verdict inputs-not-equalized, operand candidate.

§

LogNotRetained

An operand passed, or failed after a strict matched comparison, but did not retain exactly one nonempty first-run log (no --verify-log-dir, no readable directory, an empty log, or not exactly one), or the caller refused its row because it did not retain the verify logs the caller requires (ParityRejection::LogNotRetained).

§

LogUnreadable

An operand’s single retained log exists but cannot be resolved, read or hashed. Verdict reference-missing or candidate-missing.

§

NoResultRow

An operand has no usable result row: none in the run, a history crate::runner::cell_result_after_retries refuses, or a row the caller refused as missing or invalid (ParityRejection::MissingRow, ParityRejection::InvalidRow), or a PASS that used only the stripped comparator, which is below L2.

§

LogDiffFailed

The parity tool could not produce a usable comparison of two deterministic, retained logs. Three cases: (1) hermit log-diff returned a non-verdict report – NoResult, Refused, NoComparableMessages or IdenticalSoFar (IdenticalSoFar should be impossible in one-shot mode, so seeing it means a tool defect); (2) a matched or diverged report failed LogDiffReport::require_cross_backend_evidence; (3) a report ParityRecord::from_comparison cannot record. Every way the comparison itself fails to run or report (its deadline, its output directory, its paths, the spawn, its time bound, an unreadable or contradictory report, a report over the wrong inputs) is also this class. Verdict unavailable, no operand, no credit; in the floor as 0.

§

InvalidTestId

The test id is not a plain relative path, so no golden can be written below it. No operand.

§

GoldenNotWritten

The reference’s golden log or its sidecar could not be written. Operand reference.

§

EpochNotShared

The operands cannot be shown to share a HERMIT_EPOCH: operand the side that recorded none when exactly one did not, no operand when both recorded different values or neither recorded one.

§

RecordMissing

The run owed the cell a record and wrote none. Only verdict record-missing, no operand; a record never carries it.
