The shards of the local HERMIT_INTEGRATION_TAG, as (job, first binary, estimated seconds).

Until 2026-10-15 its binaries ran one test at a time, under nextest’s former hermit-serialized group, so the node’s wall time was the sum of its tests’ times: on 2bf536af the 407 tests took 312.5 s of a 312.6 s nextest run, and the node ended the DAG’s portable tail at +723 s, 327 s after it started. Each shard now runs eight tests at a time (its width channel is NEXTEST_TEST_THREADS): every guest these binaries build under CARGO_TARGET_TMPDIR goes to a <name>-<pid> path, so two test processes of one binary never overwrite each other’s program.

Each shard takes a contiguous range of binary names, from its first binary up to the next shard’s. Every shard runs the authored command, with its prepared selection, and narrows the authored filter to its own binaries, so a binary runs in exactly one shard. A binary added to the node joins the shard whose range holds its name; the shard’s NEXTEST_EXPECTED_COUNTS entry then fails until it is updated. The hosted twin is made before this split and runs the whole node.

The ranges and estimates are the per-binary sums of the PASS times of the 2bf536af and 656b74d4 runs, averaged: 66, 123 and 125 s. One shard: its job name, its first binary (None for the first shard) and its estimated seconds.
