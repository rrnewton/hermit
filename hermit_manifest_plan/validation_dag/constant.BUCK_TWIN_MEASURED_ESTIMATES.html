Buck import twins whose import is not about a second, as (tag, estimated seconds, workers). The others take 1-2 s at one worker. At the former 1 s for every twin, the dispatch order ranked e2e.manifest_c_programs_buck, which full-scorecard.compatibility_buck waits for, below every ready test node.

The width is the import’s parity post-pass: one hermit log-diff per compared cell (1,415 for c-programs, 1,070 for compat), which runs as many at once as --jobs allows; the twins’ counterparts already declare jobs_flag --jobs. Measured 2026-10-17 on the rows of validate-claude-coord-62374347d461-1792248733030795509-314424-be3b3567, whose ingest was rerun into a private import directory: c-programs took 28.8 s at –jobs 1 (30.5 s in that validation), 8.6 s at 8 and 7.1 s at 16; compat 11.1 s at 1 and 4.1 s at 8; system-utils 3.4 s at 1 and 1.8 s at 8. Every width published the same 1,498, 544 and 126 PASS rows and the same parity population and credit (c-programs 224 full, 946 partial, 245 zero). The c-programs cgroup peaked at 0.60 GiB at one worker and 0.73 GiB at eight, inside the twin’s 1-GiB baseline; it used 32.7 and 35.3 CPU-s.
