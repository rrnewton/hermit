The shards of PMU_INTEGRATION_TAG, as (job, first binary, estimated seconds).

It runs the PMU-only cases of three binaries, one test at a time, and ended the DAG on e9473c43: +460 s to +669 s (209 s), after test.hermit_integration ran in shards. Its e9473c43 PASS lines give deadline_slice_cap 114.9 s (36 tests), external_signal_interrupt 78.1 s (27) and chaos_sched_yield_progress 4.6 s (1). The estimates are the medians of 38 full validations from 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-17 (141 s and 98 s; the shards gained cases since e9473c43).
