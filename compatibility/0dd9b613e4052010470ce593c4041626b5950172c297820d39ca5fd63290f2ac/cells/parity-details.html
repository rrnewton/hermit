Parity (measured after determinism)

Parity compares a backend's log with ptrace's after both pass the repeatability check. The compared backends are kvm, liteinst, sabre, dbt; their test inputs must match. A full log match scores 1. Otherwise, the score is the matching start of the logs divided by the length of the longer log. A cell without repeatable logs has no parity score and is excluded from the mean and floor. These scores come from the saved parity summary, checked against its run records. Download the summary and recorded comparisons.
