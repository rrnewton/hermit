Hermit’s ptrace replay tool, Replayer, and the one place its Detcore instantiation is rooted.

detcore::Detcore<Replayer> drives reverie_ptrace‘s TracedTask, and that instantiation is large. In its own crate it builds in a separate rustc process, beside the other backends’ instantiations. The entry point, spawn_tracer, is not generic and returns a boxed future, so hermit instantiates nothing of it (see hermit-record for why it is boxed).
