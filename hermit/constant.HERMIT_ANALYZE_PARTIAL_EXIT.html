Expand description

hermit analyze stopped at its own budget (--time-budget, --trial-budget) and reported its best answer so far: 120.

The analysis neither failed (125) nor found the critical events (0, or --success-exit-code): its report says that it is partial, why, and how to continue it, after the stderr line HERMIT_ANALYZE_PARTIAL class=time-budget|trial-budget. It is not 124, the status of every deadline that kills what it bounds (docs/TIMEOUT_LADDER.md), because nothing is killed and the result is an answer, not a stopped run. Its row in the exit-code allocation table below carves it out of the guest’s range. Like every hermit status it can collide with a guest’s own, but analyze never exits with a guest’s status: the class line is the discriminator.
