pub const HERMIT_ANALYZE_PRECONDITION_EXIT: i32 = 2;

Exit status of hermit analyze and hermit bisect when their inputs do not give them what they need: 2.

The target run does not meet the target criteria, the baseline does not miss it or does not finish, no continuation allows a prefix search, or bisect’s endpoints are identical or its search established no exact one-swap pair of schedules with opposite outcomes between them. Analyze concluded that, and says what to change; Hermit did not fail, so it is not 125, and no fail-closed policy refused a run, so it is not 122 (which analyze exits with when Hermit refuses one of its runs). It completes the convention HERMIT_VERIFICATION_DIVERGENCE_EXIT follows, that of diff, cmp and grep: 0 an answer, 1 a difference, 2 trouble doing what was asked. clap’s usage error is 2 as well, and the class line HERMIT_ANALYZE_PRECONDITION class=... on stderr tells the two apart.

Neither command passes a guest’s status through, so for them 2 is not a guest’s; for hermit run it still is (see the allocation table below).
