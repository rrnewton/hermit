pub const SEEDED_MAX_TIMESLICE_PIECES: [(f64, u64, u64); 3];
Expand description

The pieces of the drawn maximum’s distribution, in order: each is (share, low, high), the share of seeds whose maximum falls in [low, high] virtual nanoseconds, log-uniformly within it. The pieces are contiguous, run from SEEDED_MAX_TIMESLICE_MIN_NS to SEEDED_MAX_TIMESLICE_MAX_NS, and their shares sum to one.
