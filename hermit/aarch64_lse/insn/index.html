hermit::aarch64_lse

Module insn 

Source
Expand description

The subset of A64 this rewriter decodes, and the instructions it emits.

Decoding is deliberately partial and total: every 32-bit word maps to a variant, and everything outside the subset becomes Insn::Other, which no idiom accepts. A word is only ever rewritten when every instruction of its sequence decoded into a known variant, so an unrecognised encoding can never be rewritten by accident.

Field positions follow the Arm Architecture Reference Manual for A-profile (DDI 0487, issue L.a): C6.2 for the instruction encodings, C3.2.6 “Load- Exclusive and Store-Exclusive” for the exclusive pair, and C3.2.12 “Atomic instructions” for the FEAT_LSE operations this rewriter emits.
